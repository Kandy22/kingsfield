## What that means in plain English

- We help you organize, research, and pressure-test your own thinking.
- We do not tell you what to do in your case.
- We are not your lawyer. We can't be your lawyer. We don't want to be your lawyer.

## When you actually need a licensed attorney

You should consult a licensed attorney in your jurisdiction when:

- You are facing criminal charges of any kind.
- You are facing a deprivation of liberty (involuntary commitment, deportation, child custody loss, etc.).
- You are sued for an amount you cannot afford to lose.
- You are entering a contract with significant ongoing obligations.
- You are forming a business entity.
- You are negotiating an estate, trust, or probate matter.
- The other side has a lawyer and you do not.

In all of these situations, "I used an open-source tool" is not a substitute for licensed counsel. Use Kingsfield to be a more informed client of a real lawyer. Don't use it to replace one.

## Unauthorized practice of law

In most jurisdictions, only licensed attorneys may give legal advice or represent another person in court. Kingsfield is not for use in giving advice to another person. If you are a non-lawyer using Kingsfield to help someone other than yourself, you may be engaged in unauthorized practice of law, and you assume that risk.

If you are a paralegal, advisor, researcher, or other operator working under attorney supervision, fine. If you are running an underground "law office" without a license, you are courting prosecution and we want no part of it.

## Citation accuracy

The four-gate verification pipeline is best-effort. It significantly reduces the risk of hallucinated citations, but it does not eliminate it. Free-tier currency checks are not a substitute for paid Shepard's / KeyCite. Before any high-stakes filing, supplement with paid services.

We expressly disclaim warranty that any cited authority is current, controlling, on-point, or correctly stated. Verify everything yourself before you sign your name to it.
