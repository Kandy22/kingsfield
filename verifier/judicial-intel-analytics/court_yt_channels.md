## yt-dlp Commands

```bash
# FL 2nd DCA — full archive
yt-dlp --write-info-json --write-auto-sub --sub-lang en \
  -o "%(channel)s/%(upload_date)s_%(id)s_%(title)s.%(ext)s" \
  https://www.youtube.com/@seconddistrictcourtofappea8708

# NY Court of Appeals
yt-dlp --write-info-json --write-auto-sub --sub-lang en \
  -o "%(channel)s/%(upload_date)s_%(id)s_%(title)s.%(ext)s" \
  https://www.youtube.com/@newyorkstatecourtofappeals7445

# Metadata only (no video download — just index first)
yt-dlp --flat-playlist --dump-single-json \
  https://www.youtube.com/@seconddistrictcourtofappea8708 > fl_2dca_index.json
```
