# 29 factual categories (one sentence each)

## T1 — State Trial Court Intelligence

### M1 Subjective and Professional History
1. **Career and Employment History** — Tracks the judge’s prior legal roles and practice environments (prosecutor, defender, firm, bench) as a path, not a slogan.
2. **Educational Background** — Identifies law school, undergraduate, and clerkship ties that often predict local procedural culture.
3. **Professional and Political Affiliations** — Lists bar sections, Inns of Court, policy groups, and partisan offices the judge has publicly associated with.
4. **Practical Chambers Data** — Publishes clerk phone, department, standing orders, and courtroom rules a filer actually needs.

### M2 High-Level Metrics
5. **Active Cases** — Counts matters currently pending on the judge’s docket at the snapshot date.
6. **Average Case Length** — Reports the typical time a case sits before this judge from filing to close.
7. **Verdict Breakdown** — Shows the statistical mix of bench-trial outcomes (plaintiff, defense, mixed, directed) for this judge.
8. **Case Practice Area Breakdown** — Splits the docket by broad practice area such as torts, commercial, property, or labor and employment.
9. **Matter Type Breakdown** — Splits the same docket into granular matter types such as eminent domain or wrongful termination.

### M3 Granular Motion Practice
10. **Motion-by-Motion Success Rates** — Gives grant, denial, and partial-grant rates across 40-plus motion types (demurrer, summary judgment, in limine, and the rest).
11. **Comparative Motion Benchmarks** — Sets the judge’s motion rates next to county and state averages so outliers are visible.

### M4 Case Dispositions
12. **Disposition Breakdown** — Tracks how this judge’s cases end: dismissal, settlement, jury verdict, consolidation, or removal.

### M5 Timing Analytics
13. **Average Case Duration** — Average days from filing to disposition, ignoring how the case ended.
14. **Average Time to Trial** — Average days from filing to the first day of trial before this judge.
15. **Average Time to First Dismissal Order** — Average days to the first dismissal order, used as a rough statistical proxy for settlement timing.

## T2 — Enterprise and Federal Court Analytics

16. **State Court Motion Metrics** — Document-driven motion-practice and timing stats for 40-plus motion types on state dockets, keyed by judge.
17. **Timing Events** — Algorithmic forecasts of when a matter is likely to hit key litigation milestones in this forum.
18. **Legal Findings** — Practice-specific holdings mapped onto the judgment events that produced them.
19. **Appeals Analytics** — Reversal rates, rehearing outcomes, and Supreme Court history for this judge’s or this court’s appellate trail.
20. **Class Action Analytics** — Summarizes settlement dollars, fee awards, and class-representative awards to bound a litigation budget.
21. **Entity Profiling** — Comparative case histories for opposing counsel, firms, parties, experts, and courts against this judge.

## T3 — Computational and Predictive Modeling

22. **Approximating a Judge** — Fits binary decision trees and if-then rules that approximate the splits this judge appears to apply to stated facts.
23. **Synthetic Legal Precedent Structures** — Builds layered citation graphs from feature distance and Lévy-flight-style jumps among like-featured prior cases.
24. **The Duty to Settle** — Computes a reasonable settlement zone from policy limits, coverage probability, costs, and attachable assets.
25. **Jury and Voting Models** — Runs panel-probability math (Condorcet jury theorem, Banzhaf index) for multi-member courts and juries.
26. **Judicial Outcome Prediction** — Estimates odds of early rulings such as motions to dismiss and summary judgment; vendor-claimed accuracy is not Kingsfield’s claim.
27. **Litigation Timelines** — Projects duration bands for pre-discovery, discovery, and trial phases in this forum before this judge.
28. **Forum and Strategy Optimization** — Surfaces data on motion timing, forum choice, and expert selection given comparable past matters.
29. **Counsel-Performance Analytics** — Benchmarks how named attorneys fare, how long they take, and how they finish in front of this specific judge.
