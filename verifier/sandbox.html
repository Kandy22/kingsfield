How to use this tool

  1. Load your data — click "📂 Load File" above or the button in the header.
  2. Pick an entry from the left sidebar. Colored dots show automated status.
  3. Read the quote (panel ①) — what Showalter claims the case says.
  4. Read the passage (panel ②) — pulled from the actual opinion. Does it match?
  5. Open the opinion on CourtListener, hit Cmd+F, paste the quote.
  6. Set your verdict (panel ③): ✓ Verified, ✗ Wrong, or ~ Unsure.
  7. Export when done — your annotated JSON becomes the ground truth dataset.
