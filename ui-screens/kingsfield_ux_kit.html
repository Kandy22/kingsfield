11 Chat UI

User: amber right. AI: elevated card left. Citations inline in mono. Never truncate a citation string.

What's the standard for dismissal under Rule 12(b)(6)?
Under Rule 12(b)(6), a complaint survives if it states a claim plausible on its face — enough factual content to allow the court to draw the reasonable inference of liability.

Does this apply differently in the 9th Circuit?
The 9th Circuit applies Iqbal/Twombly uniformly, with some elaboration on the plausibility standard in complex cases.
