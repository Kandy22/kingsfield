# First, Kill All the Lawyers.
## A note on the Sullivan & Cromwell hallucination letter, which I have read so you don't have to.

*Monday.*

Sullivan & Cromwell filed an apology letter in bankruptcy court on April 18th explaining that four of the cases they cited in a Chapter 15 motion were imaginary. Hallucinated. Generated by a machine that does not know what a case is, attached to a brief signed by a partner who apparently doesn't either.

The letter is three pages. I have read it. I have annotated it. I have, in two places, written the word *no* in pencil. The dog has been walked twice. My assistant left at two.

S&C's letter is structurally remarkable. It opens by defining the word *hallucination* for the federal judge it is addressed to. Chief Judge Martin Glenn. Of the Southern District. Who supervised the GM bankruptcy. The letter contains a glossary entry. For him. As if he might otherwise wonder.

It then explains that the firm has comprehensive policies on the use of AI in legal work, and that these policies "were not followed." It then explains that the firm also has comprehensive policies on manual citation review, and that those policies were *also* not followed. The two sentences are placed next to each other. Whoever drafted the letter did not notice that the second one converts the first one from a procedural reference into a confession.

The remedial measures listed at the bottom include "additional training," which is BigLaw for *we did nothing.*

Here is the part nobody at S&C will say in writing: **the firm did not catch this. The other side caught it. The judge caught it.** The four-gate verification protocol Sullivan & Cromwell has built into its $1,800-an-hour pricing model is, in operating reality, *opposing counsel reading the brief.*

I have spent forty years in this profession. I have watched it survive the photocopier, the fax, email, the BlackBerry, e-discovery, and a non-trivial number of indictments. The AI is not what's killing it. The AI is just the speed at which the killing now arrives.

What S&C should have written, and didn't, and won't:

---

> SULLIVAN & CROMWELL LLP
> 125 Broad Street, New York
>
> *Dear Chief Judge Glenn:*
>
> We filed bullshit. We did not know it was bullshit because we did not read it. We did not read it because we paid a machine to read it, and the machine, which has been confidently bullshitting since 2022, bullshitted us. We have undertaken remedial measures, which consist primarily of being caught.
>
> A list of the cases we cited that do not exist is attached as Schedule A.
>
> A list of the cases we cited that *do* exist but stand for the opposite of what we said they stand for is attached as Schedule B.
>
> Schedule B is longer.
>
> Respectfully,
> /s/ A Name You'll Recognize
>
> cc: Our insurance carrier · Our other insurance carrier · The author of our 2024 White Paper on Responsible AI Use, included as a reminder

---

The associate who drafted the brief is gone. The partner who signed it is at the Amagansett house this weekend. There is a reason for this. There has always been a reason for this. The technology changed. The reason did not.

*First*, said the man in the play, *kill all the lawyers.*

He wasn't calling for our destruction. He was identifying the bottleneck.

The play is *Henry VI, Part 2.* The character is a butcher. He's literally a butcher. Shakespeare named him *Dick the Butcher.* And four hundred years later we're still arguing about whether he was being mean.

He wasn't. Read the scene. He was being practical.
