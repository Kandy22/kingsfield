What We Are

A capability equalizer. The same research, verification, and strategic analysis BigLaw charges $800/hour for — delivered without a retainer, without gatekeeping, without the theater.

The law is public, written, and belongs to everyone. Kingsfield is the bypass on the toll road the Bar built around it.

What We Are Not

Not a lawyer replacement. Not a legal advice service. Not apologetic about our existence. Not Westlaw with a chatbot bolted on.

This is not a tool. This is a weapon. The skull is not decorative.

Light Mode Note

Kingsfield is dark-first. A light mode may be offered as a toggle but should never be the default — the black communicates authority, not a missing feature. If a light treatment is needed (print, certain embeds), invert: #0a0a0a text on #f5f5f0 background, gold stays gold.

Logo & Mark
The Wordmark.

The wordmark is Big Shoulders Display Black, uppercase, tracking -0.01em. "Kingsfield" is the surname; the entire product name is "Kingsfield Lawfare." Do not abbreviate to "KL" or "K." The skull is the brand mark — it is always used at full fidelity or not at all.

Skull Mark Usage Rules
The baroque skull is the visual identity anchor. Use it at hero scale (full bleed or large viewport percentage) — never as a small icon or favicon. At small sizes, use the wordmark alone. The skull always faces right (as in the hero image). The ornamental overlay (baroque floral/laurel) is inseparable from the skull — never use a plain skull. When placing on any background other than black, ensure the skull image has sufficient contrast.
✓ Logo Do's
  • Use the full wordmark + skull in hero contexts
  • Use wordmark alone in nav and small contexts
  • Allow negative space around the mark
  • Gold accent can be on either "Kings" or "field" or "Lawfare"
  • The dividing line in the nav: Kingsfield [space] Lawfare
✗ Logo Don'ts
  • Abbreviate to "KL" or just "K"
  • Use any skull other than the baroque version
  • Place the skull as a small favicon or 32px icon
  • Use the wordmark in sentence case (kingsfield)
  • Apply gradients, drop shadows, or color fills to the skull

The Chat Screen.

The key differentiator Harvey can't copy: the Four-Gate display, the Skeptic veto card, and the LLM Council running visible to the user.

Is there a negligence per se argument in my case given the cell phone violation?

Yes. VC § 23123.5 prohibits handheld wireless device use while driving. Your AT&T records (Exhibit E) confirm an active call at the time of collision — this establishes the statutory violation. Under CACI 418, violation of a safety statute constitutes negligence per se if the plaintiff belongs to the class the statute was designed to protect and the harm resulted from the violation. All four verification gates passed.

Imagery Direction
What We Show.

Imagery should feel like evidence. Nothing stock. Nothing aspirational-lifestyle. The visual world is: institutional weight, material reality, the system's machinery. The skull and ornament are the only decorative elements — everything else earns its place by being real.

🏛️
Institutions
Courthouses, law libraries, government buildings — the system's physical form
💵
Money & Power
Currency, scales, gavels — the mechanisms of access and denial
👁️
Surveillance / Attention
The red eye (already in your assets) — seeing through the system
⚙️
Machinery
Mechanisms, gears, industrial weight — the law as a machine that can be operated
✓ Image Do's
  • High contrast, dark or desaturated photos
  • Red/monochrome color grading for editorial intensity
  • Images that feel like evidence or documentation
  • Textures: paper, stone, metal, concrete
  • The skull+ornament at hero scale only
✗ Image Don'ts
  • Handshakes, conference rooms, laptops
  • Smiling professionals in suits
  • Generic "scales of justice" clip art
  • Bright, saturated lifestyle photography
  • AI-generated people or faces
Assets Already In Your Folder — Use These
The red owl eye, the dollar bill macro, "The Cathedral's Last Breath" smoke image, the "No Vaseline" jar — all four of these in your mockups are exactly right. They feel like content, not decoration. Continue in this direction. Every blog card image should feel like it was selected from a case file.
